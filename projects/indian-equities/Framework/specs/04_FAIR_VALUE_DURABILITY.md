# Long-Term Fair-Value Durability Specification

## Question
How reliable and durable is the company's intrinsic value over a full cycle?

## Required inputs
- Normalized ROIC versus WACC
- FCF conversion
- Reinvestment runway
- Balance-sheet leverage/liquidity
- Competitive advantage / market share stability
- Revenue and margin volatility
- Terminal-value share of DCF
- Capital allocation history
- Governance / dilution / minority leakage where relevant

## Subscores
- ROIC/WACC quality: 20%
- FCF conversion: 15%
- Reinvestment runway: 20%
- Balance sheet: 15%
- Moat / competitive durability: 15%
- Capital allocation/governance: 10%
- Model terminal-value dependence: 5%

## Score anchors
5.0 = highly durable compounder, high ROIC, strong FCF, low model fragility
4.0 = durable with one meaningful caveat
3.0 = average durability / cyclical but financially sound
2.0 = weak cash conversion, leverage, cyclicality or model dependence
1.0 = fragile economics / poor capital allocation
0.0 = material permanent impairment risk

## Guardrails
- High growth without FCF does not score highly by itself.
- Terminal value >70% of EV creates an explicit confidence penalty.
- New/unproven businesses require probability haircuts rather than mature-business certainty.
