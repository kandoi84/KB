# SBI thesis tracker — 28 September 2026

## Stance (under 100 words)

**Watch / avoid a new valuation-led buy at ₹983.00 (25 September NSE close).** The probability-weighted fair value is ₹840.03, 14.54% below price. The most-likely path assumes 17% RoE for five years, then 14% as the bank matures. Price appears to require roughly 15.5% terminal RoE on that same basis. The market may expect stronger earnings and durable low credit losses. Evidence is mixed: Q1 loan growth and asset quality were strong, while deposits lagged loans. Confirm official price and reconcile book value before raising conviction. [Model](Valuation/2026-09-25.md).

This is a bank-specific observation, not an assertion about the whole sector. A current RBI and peer dataset is needed before labeling the sector trough, mid-cycle or peak. No verified consensus or revision history was obtained, so “easy” versus “demanding” market estimates remains **unknown**. ## What differs from the price

The model's central terminal RoE is 14%; the same model needs about 15.5% to reach the observed price, holding its other assumptions fixed. The investment question is whether SBI can defend a higher long-run return as deposit competition and credit losses normalize. The September investor-day presentation describes data-led underwriting and technology advantages, but those are management claims to test against deposit costs, credit cohorts and peer results. [SBI investor-day presentation, pp. 8–10](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17836/53469043/SBI_Investor_Day_FINAL_INR_.pdf/5e1d32dc-3d19-74c8-6e63-a05e850db58e?t=1789402284983).
