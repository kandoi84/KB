## What matters next

1. **Funding:** Check whether deposit growth catches up with credit growth. Current loan growth cannot simply be extended unchanged in the model.
2. **Profit quality:** Test whether credit cost stays low without weakening new loan cohorts. The 0.27% quarterly credit cost is a starting observation, not a permanent assumption.
3. **Margins:** Watch whether the Q1 2.86% whole-bank NIM stabilizes as deposit costs and loan yields reset.
4. **Capital:** Track whether 15.67% capital adequacy supports growth without further dilution.
5. **Subsidiaries:** SBI's Q1 deck reports an SBI Funds Management stake change following a July 2026 IPO. Refresh ownership before any sum-of-the-parts work; the Q1 table uses June stakes while its footnote gives a later post-IPO stake.
