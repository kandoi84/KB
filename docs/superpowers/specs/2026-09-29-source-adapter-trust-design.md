## Decision and activation gate

Keep `reviewed_local_primary_v1` from mini spec 07 as the only executable acquisition route. The next slice is an **observed-gap and permission ledger**, a deterministic route decision, and diagnostic trust reporting. No general crawler, NSE/BSE website scraper, inferred endpoint, or live HTTP connector is approved by this spec. Promote one narrowly scoped connector only in a separate reviewed change after a real open gap shows that the local route is insufficient and written endpoint-specific rights permit the exact automated method. A public download link, a free price, an open-source wrapper, or an MCP description does not establish those rights.

The working tree search on 2026-09-29 found no real `gap_attempts`, claim reports, or source readiness reports under the project/runtime state paths. Synthetic fixtures prove contract behavior, not an observed production gap. Therefore adapter expansion remains **dormant** until the activation packet below exists. This is the smallest useful interpretation of program 13's “only for observed data gaps and credible calibration samples” condition.

Mini spec 07 already freezes gap classification, exact source identity, rights attestations, attempted bytes, immutable source versions, retries, and `publication_allowed: false`. Spec 13 must call or inspect that contract, not create another source store or claim closer. The old cutoff and report remain immutable; new evidence enters only a later refresh and 05 human review.

## Activation packet and route contract

An activation packet binds one **real** `OPEN` gap and includes: exact claim/source report IDs and digests, source and claim request digests, entity ISIN, cutoff, gap ID/reason/classification, the failed or stale preferred source, and one proposed authoritative document. It records why reviewed local acquisition cannot meet the gap, including attempt ID/status where available. A reviewer names the source authority, source kind, URL/endpoint, format, applicable terms or agreement reference, allowed actor, automated/manual method, frequency, storage and retention, research purpose, redistribution limits, rights reviewer and review time. Endpoint, DNS/redirect scope, parser/version, size and rate limits, retry policy, and required metadata are versioned in the packet. Rights review is a human attestation bound to this exact scope; it is not derived from URL reachability.

`select_gap_route` takes that packet and the intact frozen parents. It reuses 07's parent semantics and classification. It returns a digest-bound **plan** only: `REVIEWED_LOCAL`, `RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `NO_APPROVED_ROUTE`, or `HUMAN_WORK_REQUIRED`, with `gap_status: OPEN` and `publication_allowed: false`. It cannot download or register a source. Only `PUBLIC_PRIMARY_MISSING` and `PUBLIC_PRIMARY_STALE` from a genuine missing/stale report may receive an acquisition route. Internal, proprietary, unclassified, linked, damaged, after-cutoff, or already-supported gaps cannot become web work by relabeling. Selection is by exact authority/document and approved capability, never by broad search or an LLM-generated URL. An empty real-gap ledger produces no adapter-expansion plan.

When a future connector is separately approved, it receives one frozen exact request from the route plan and uses 07's intent/result and `record_source` path. It must record: adapter ID/version, rights receipt digest, attempted URL, request method and timestamp, response status/content type/length, redirects and final host, retry count and wait, server rate-limit headers or documented allowance, error class, retrieval timestamp, bytes hash, source version ID, and source publication/observation dates with provenance of each date. Never record credentials or raw access tokens. Conditional requests and deduplication are allowed only if the source agreement allows them. Any 401/403, 429, robots/terms change, identity mismatch, unexpected redirect, oversized or malformed body, missing publication date, or rights expiry stops the route with an explicit blocked/error receipt; no browser-cookie or proxy bypass. Retries have a bounded documented count and respect `Retry-After`; no retry changes an old cutoff. A 200 response alone is not validated evidence.

## Trust observations and calibration

“Trust” describes the **evidence workflow**, not whether a company is attractive. Every observation is append-only and points to an immutable attempt, source/report IDs, adapter version, exact bytes hash, adjudicator ID, adjudication time, rubric version, and outcome. A reviewer other than the adapter operator checks a sample of both successful and blocked attempts against the raw document and authoritative publication page or permitted source record. Sampling is stratified by adapter, authority, document type, issuer, period, and failure status; selection seed and excluded population are recorded so a convenient-only sample cannot masquerade as representative.

Report separate numerators/denominators for: source/ISIN and document identity accuracy; publication-date and cutoff accuracy; raw hash and replay reproducibility; rights/workflow compliance; usable document yield; and classified failure rates (permission, rate limit, transport, parse, identity, stale, missing). Also report missing provenance fields and median/time-range acquisition lag. For each binary rate show a 95% Wilson interval and cohort dates. Never collapse them into one unqualified trust score. A disputed label is excluded from the accuracy numerator and shown as unresolved, with count; it is not silently treated as a pass. An operator change, parser change, rights change, or source policy change starts a new cohort.

`INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE` applies to any adapter-specific accuracy or compliance estimate with fewer than 30 independently adjudicated attempts, fewer than five issuers, or fewer than two reporting periods; these are conservative **reporting gates**, not claims of statistical validity. The report must still show raw counts and failures. Zero-denominator metrics are `NO_ESTIMATE`. A probability-calibration plot or expected-calibration-error statistic is `NOT_CALIBRATABLE` until the workflow emits frozen ex ante probabilities and later independent labels; current 07 attempts do not. No trust result feeds poker scores, fair value, case promotion, 12 investment calibration, or publication. A future use of trust in investment scoring requires a separate reviewed spec and historical evaluation; a high operational success rate cannot substitute for investment evidence.

## Acceptance and failure cases

- With no observed real eligible gap, selection offers no new executable adapter; fixture-only data is labeled synthetic.
- An exact real eligible packet with local rights selects 07; one missing a scoped rights permission records `RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED` without fetching. Internal/restricted/damaged/cross-entity/forged-parent packets fail closed.
- A changed parent, packet, rights scope, or adapter version under the same route ID fails immutable replay. A later source stays after the old cutoff; every plan and receipt keeps the gap open and publication blocked.
- An approved future connector must prove bounded rate/error provenance, exact response identity, safe redirects, no credential leakage, and idempotent retry before activation. A forbidden or ambiguous response cannot enter the source registry.
- Trust reports show counts, cohort definitions, uncertainty and `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`/`NO_ESTIMATE`/`NOT_CALIBRATABLE` states. Synthetic fixtures cannot satisfy the real-sample reporting gate, and no trust field enters investment scores.
