# Guardrails

1. No score without as-of date.
2. No factor score without source-backed inputs or explicit missing-data penalty.
3. No company can receive Evidence Grade A with material analyst-assumption dependence.
4. No valuation factor 5/5 if market price is inside the fair-value uncertainty band.
5. No durability score >4 if FCF conversion is structurally weak without a demonstrated reason.
6. No sector score >4 when the sector is at peak margins unless supply/demand evidence supports persistence.
7. No catalyst score >4 from a single event with probability <70%.
8. No high-conviction ranking if score error band >±8 points.
9. If two top-5 candidates overlap heavily on one macro factor, apply portfolio-level correlation review.
10. Material new information requires re-scoring affected factors before using stale rank.
11. Poker labels cannot override raw score, error band or evidence grade.
12. Historical evals must be strictly point-in-time.
