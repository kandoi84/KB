# Earnings Expectations Specification

## Question
Are current market earnings expectations easy, fair or demanding, and are revisions likely to rise or fall?

## Required inputs
- Current consensus revenue/EBITDA/EPS for next 8 quarters and FY1/FY2 where available
- Dispersion of estimates
- 1m/3m/6m revision trends
- Company guidance
- Key operating KPI assumptions implicit in consensus
- Prior surprise history
- Sector read-throughs

## Output
- Direction: Falling / Stable / Rising
- Difficulty: Easy / Fair / Demanding
- Revision probability: probability of >5% FY1 EPS/EBITDA revision up/down over next 6 months
- Confidence grade

## Guardrails
- Never call expectations 'easy' without identifying the operating assumption the market is underestimating.
- Separate cyclical spot conditions from sustainable forecast changes.
- Do not use stale consensus without an as-of timestamp.
