# SBI valuation — first pilot anchor

**Model date:** 25 September 2026 close; prepared 28 September 2026. **Price:** ₹983.00 (NSE close, two secondary sources). **Probability-weighted fair value:** ₹840.03 per share; **gap to price:** −14.54%. **Most-likely cell:** ₹829.02. **Model range:** ₹521.92–₹1,282.49 across the nine defined cells. This is a scenario range, **not** a statistical confidence interval. [Price sources](../Sources/LEDGER.md#open-source-checks), [reproducible model](../Model/valuation.py).

## Why an equity model

Bank deposits and borrowing are operating inputs, so a conventional industrial-company free-cash-flow-to-firm model can misstate value. This pilot values the cash that can ultimately go to shareholders. It starts from **standalone** SBI capital plus reserves and uses standalone profitability; it does not add subsidiary market values a second time.

The Q1FY27 deck reports standalone capital of ₹923 crore and reserves/surplus of ₹5,65,908 crore at 30 June 2026. A prior SBI disclosure gives 9,230,617,586 shares after the July 2025 placement. The model's approximate starting book equity per share is `(923 + 565908) / 923.0617586 = ₹614.08`. The presentation rounds balance-sheet figures to whole crores. [SBI Q1FY27 presentation, p. 46](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17836/1275616/07082026_SBI%2BAnalyst%2BPresentation%2BQ1FY27.pdf/6e4ca530-1666-574e-22fb-f9e19d553ef5?t=1786091443352); [SBI placement note](https://home.sbi.bank.in/documents/17826/17948/Financial%2BResults_Q2FY26_Standalone.pdf/Financial%20Results_Q2FY26_Standalonea975.pdf?t=1762245153677).

**Definition warning:** SBI's own five-year highlights call FY26 BVPS ₹512.31, while FY26 capital plus reserves divided by shares gives a different number. This pilot uses its explicitly defined capital-plus-reserves basis throughout, but the difference must be reconciled before calling the anchor fully investment-grade. [SBI five-year highlights, Tables III, IV](https://sbi.bank.in/en/web/investor-relations/sbi-financial-highlights-past-5).

## Most-likely operating path

| Input | Value | Evidence and status |
|---|---:|---|
| Starting standalone equity/share | ₹614.08 | Derived from reported rounded June 2026 balances and placement shares |
| Annual RoE, years 1–5 | 17.0% | Analyst estimate; below FY26 reported 18.57% and Q1FY27 17.87% |
| Dividend payout, years 1–5 | 20.0% | Analyst estimate; near SBI's reported FY26 payout ratio of 20.01% |
| Terminal RoE | 14.0% | Analyst normalization; main durability debate |
| Long-run book growth | 7.0% | Analyst assumption, not a GDP forecast or guidance |
| Required return on equity | 13.0% | Analyst assumption; risk and rate debate |

Reported anchors: [SBI five-year highlights](https://sbi.bank.in/en/web/investor-relations/sbi-financial-highlights-past-5), [Q1FY27 presentation, p. 5](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17836/1275616/07082026_SBI%2BAnalyst%2BPresentation%2BQ1FY27.pdf/6e4ca530-1666-574e-22fb-f9e19d553ef5?t=1786091443352). The 17% path assumes present asset quality largely holds but does not capitalize today's low credit cost forever. It is a judgment, not consensus.

Each year, `earnings = opening book × RoE`; `dividend = earnings × 20%`; and `closing book = opening book + earnings − dividend`. At year five, the terminal dividend equals `year-five book × (terminal RoE − 7% growth)`. Terminal value at year five is that dividend divided by `(required return − 7% growth)`. Discount five dividends and terminal value to today. The terminal formula implies a **50% payout** in the 14% terminal RoE case, versus 20% during years 1–5. That is an explicit assumption that mature growth releases more cash; if regulation or management retains more capital without earning the assumed return, value is lower. [Executable formula](../Model/valuation.py).

## Independent debates and probabilities

These are analyst probabilities, not measured frequencies. The two debates are treated as independent for this first pilot; common exposure to macro rates and credit conditions could make the tails fatter than shown.

1. **Profit durability:** 20% chance terminal RoE falls to 12%; 60% chance of 14%; 20% chance of 16%. Deposit competition, loan quality and subsidiary distributions drive this debate.
2. **Required return:** 20% chance of 12%; 60% chance of 13%; 20% chance of 14%. Rates, policy ownership, governance and valuation risk drive this debate.

| Terminal RoE ↓ / equity return → | 12% | 13% | 14% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 12% | ₹755.12 | ₹618.83 | ₹521.92 |
| 14% | ₹1,018.80 | **₹829.02** | ₹694.31 |
| 16% | ₹1,282.49 | ₹1,039.20 | ₹866.71 |

Multiplying each cell by its two probabilities and summing gives **₹840.03**. At ₹983.00, the market price is about **17.0% above** that expected value, or expected value is **14.54% below** price. Holding other most-likely inputs fixed, ₹983 implies roughly **15.5% terminal RoE** rather than 14%. That is an inference from this model, not a statement of market consensus.

## Cross-check and limits

- The model's June 2026 book basis gives roughly **1.60×** price/book (`983/614.08`). SBI reports **1.91×** at its FY26 year-end price of ₹979.40 on its own BVPS convention. The dates and definitions differ, so the numbers cannot be read as a multiple compression series. [SBI five-year highlights](https://sbi.bank.in/en/web/investor-relations/sbi-financial-highlights-past-5).
- In the most-likely case, about **89% of value comes from the terminal value**, so small changes to long-run RoE, growth or the required return move the answer sharply. The ₹840 figure is a model output, not a precise target.
- Subsidiary value is **not** separately attributed. SBI Life, SBI Card and SBI Funds Management may affect the parent through earnings, dividends and the carrying value of investments. A detailed sum-of-the-parts bridge is a future check, not an added premium today. [SBI Q1FY27 presentation, pp. 41–43](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17836/1275616/07082026_SBI%2BAnalyst%2BPresentation%2BQ1FY27.pdf/6e4ca530-1666-574e-22fb-f9e19d553ef5?t=1786091443352).
- A single dated price and fair-value anchor cannot produce legitimate ±1σ or ±2σ trading bands. Build a point-in-time history first.
- No verified consensus, next result date, peer multiple series or official NSE bhavcopy was captured. These gaps lower confidence; they are not filled with guesses.
