# Source Policy

## Priority
1. Exchange/company filings
2. Company IR / transcripts
3. Audited annual reports
4. High-quality market-data providers / consensus feeds
5. Credible news / industry sources
6. Analyst assumptions

## Rules
- No unsourced load-bearing datapoints.
- Every market-sensitive value must have an as-of date.
- Consensus from public aggregators must be labelled weaker than institutional consensus feeds.
- If two primary sources conflict, stop score finalization until reconciled or flag the factor as low confidence.
- No future data may enter historical snapshots used in backtests.
