# Goal: evidence that improves investment judgments

24 October 2026. Success is source-backed, reproducible analysis that identifies
an expectation gap and survives later evidence—not more folders or indicators.
No superior investment performance is claimed by meeting data targets alone.

## Working targets

For each covered company, aim for five annual periods and eight quarters of
comparable financials, sector KPIs, capital/share changes and permitted source
documents. Coverage must reflect actual availability; missing periods stay
missing. Maintain publication, first-seen and review timestamps separately.
Historical backfills are not evidence the KB possessed the data at the time.

Every load-bearing observation needs source URL/document, date, period, unit,
reporting scope, locator, hash/version and reported/derived/assumption label.
Keep flows, stock balances and annualised ratios distinct. Check comparative
figures and restatements; reconcile definitions before comparing companies.

Start with SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and TCS. Reuse existing research and
company-prepared files; do not reconstruct provider databases or manufacture
missing consensus. Extend the proven workflow sector by sector to all 50.

## Acceptance before a research conclusion

1. Actual source acquired and integrity checked; rights and identity reviewed.
2. Comparable inputs reconciled to the filing; conflicts become explicit gaps.
3. Source-to-calculation trail reproduces the result independently.
4. State what the evidence changes in the operating path or market expectation.
5. Revise value only when the economic evidence warrants it; preserve history.
6. Track subsequent outcomes, including wrong forecasts and no-change decisions.

Track acquired documents, registered versions, extracted observations,
reviewed observations, unresolved conflicts and model-ready companies separately.
Homepage access, a file hash or a source registration alone is not a verified
financial claim or an investment edge. Retain all existing publication gates.

## First actual evidence increment

SBI's real Q1FY27 presentation is registered, with 22 page-pinned comparative
observations. Its dated earnings update identifies the funding-growth gap and
the unresolved book-value bridge without revising fair value. TCS's actual
Q2FY27 workbook is acquired but awaits source-date confirmation and review.
These are initial evidence increments, not completion of the 50-company target.
