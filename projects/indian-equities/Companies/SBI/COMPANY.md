## What the bank owns and earns

SBI is a deposit-funded bank with retail, agriculture, SME, corporate and overseas lending. It also owns stakes in life insurance, cards, asset management, general insurance and other financial businesses. For this first model, **standalone** bank earnings and equity are valued together; subsidiary market values are **not** added, since dividends and investment carrying values can already affect standalone accounts. A later sum-of-the-parts model needs a clean bridge that removes those effects first. [SBI Q1FY27 presentation, pp. 9, 41–43](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17836/1275616/07082026_SBI%2BAnalyst%2BPresentation%2BQ1FY27.pdf/6e4ca530-1666-574e-22fb-f9e19d553ef5?t=1786091443352).

## Current state

SBI reported Q1FY27 standalone net interest income of ₹46,992 crore (+14.88% year on year) and net profit of ₹21,121 crore (+10.23%). Gross advances rose 18.63%, while deposits rose 9.73%. Whole-bank net interest margin was 2.86%, down 3 basis points year on year. Gross NPA was 1.47%, net NPA 0.38%, credit cost 0.27%, and capital adequacy 15.67%. These are **reported**, not forecasts. [SBI Q1FY27 presentation, pp. 5, 8](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17836/1275616/07082026_SBI%2BAnalyst%2BPresentation%2BQ1FY27.pdf/6e4ca530-1666-574e-22fb-f9e19d553ef5?t=1786091443352).

The key tension is credit growing faster than deposits. It can support near-term income, but continued divergence may raise funding costs or slow lending. The Q1 snapshot alone does not establish a durable trend. [SBI Q1FY27 presentation, pp. 8, 13–14](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/17836/1275616/07082026_SBI%2BAnalyst%2BPresentation%2BQ1FY27.pdf/6e4ca530-1666-574e-22fb-f9e19d553ef5?t=1786091443352).

## Research architecture

- **Primary method:** five-year standalone bank equity model, followed by a terminal justified price/book calculation. It explicitly models return on equity, retained earnings, dividends, required return and terminal growth.
- **Most likely path:** 17% annual return on equity for five years, 20% payout, then 14% terminal return on equity, 7% book growth and 13% required return. These are **analyst assumptions** anchored to recent reported profitability, not management guidance.
- **Independent debates:** durability of terminal return on equity and the required return for bank equity. Their probabilities are analyst judgments and feed a nine-cell valuation grid.
- **Cross-checks:** FY26 reported price/book was 1.91× at ₹979.40; the pilot's simple June 2026 standalone equity per share is about ₹614.08, giving about 1.60× at the September price. The two book-value definitions do not reconcile; see the valuation note. Do not treat the apparent multiple change as a clean historical comparison. [SBI five-year highlights](https://sbi.bank.in/en/web/investor-relations/sbi-financial-highlights-past-5).
