# ICICI Bank — Premortem
Assume the thesis disappoints over 12–24 months:
1. Current ~20% loan growth proves too aggressive and future slippages rise.
2. Easing rates eventually compress NIM more than Q1 FY27 suggests.
3. Credit costs normalize from unusually benign levels, reducing ROA.
4. High starting valuation absorbs operating upside; quality is already priced in.
5. Subsidiary/capital-allocation actions dilute rather than enhance parent-bank returns.
