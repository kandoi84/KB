# Sector Attractiveness Specification

## Question
How attractive are sector economics and sector valuation over the next 12-36 months?

## Required inputs
- Utilization/capacity balance
- Supply additions / closures
- Demand growth
- Pricing power / spreads / margins
- Competitive intensity / market structure
- Regulatory backdrop
- Sector ROIC versus WACC
- Sector valuation percentile versus history

## Subscores
- Cycle position: 25%
- Supply/demand structure: 25%
- Competitive structure: 20%
- Sector valuation: 20%
- Regulatory/technology risk: 10%

## Score anchors
5.0 = trough/early-cycle economics with improving structure and attractive valuation
4.0 = favorable economics or valuation with manageable risks
3.0 = mid-cycle / balanced
2.0 = late-cycle or deteriorating structure
1.0 = peak economics and expensive valuation / material overcapacity
0.0 = structural impairment

## Guardrails
- Peak current margins reduce, not increase, attractiveness unless durability is independently proven.
- Do not infer sector attractiveness from stock-price performance.
- Sector valuation and company valuation must be separately measured.
