# Macro Factor Specification

## Question
Is the current macro backdrop a tailwind or headwind for this company's earnings and valuation over the next 12-24 months?

## Required inputs
- Policy rate / real rate
- Bond yields / financing conditions
- Credit growth / liquidity where relevant
- INR direction and FX sensitivity
- Key commodity input/output prices
- GDP / industrial production / consumption proxy relevant to company
- Global cycle indicator where material

## Method
1. Map company sensitivities to relevant macro variables.
2. Measure current state versus 5-10 year percentile where possible.
3. Measure direction: improving / stable / deteriorating.
4. Score impact on company, not the economy generally.

## Score anchors
5.0 = broad macro tailwind, favorable percentile and improving trend
4.0 = clear net tailwind
3.0 = mixed / neutral
2.0 = clear net headwind
1.0 = severe headwind
0.0 = macro conditions threaten earnings/balance-sheet viability

## Guardrails
- Never use a generic India macro score for all companies.
- Commodity producers and consumers must have opposite sensitivity signs where appropriate.
- Separate level from direction.
- Avoid double counting sector variables already captured in sector score.

## Confidence
A: >80% inputs current and directly relevant
B: 60-80%
C: 40-60%
D: <40% or mostly narrative
