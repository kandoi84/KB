# HDFC Bank — Premortem
Assume the thesis disappoints over 12–24 months:
1. Deposit competition remains structurally intense and NIM fails to recover.
2. Balance-sheet growth stays below expectations because merger-era funding repair takes longer.
3. Asset-quality normalization lifts credit cost above the benign current level.
4. Market continues to assign a lower multiple because post-merger ROE remains below historical HDFC Bank quality.
5. Consensus already embeds most of the expected normalization; rerating never arrives.
