## Research implication, not a new forecast

Reported advance growth of 18.63% versus deposit growth of 9.73% implies an
**8.90 percentage-point growth gap** (derived subtraction of the reported rates).
NII and profit increased while whole-bank NIM declined 3 basis points.
The useful question is whether income growth can persist if funding competition
or credit costs normalize. One quarter cannot establish that outcome. Compare
subsequent filings and funding costs before revising the operating path.

This evidence supports retaining the existing funding/margin debate; it does
not resolve published BVPS versus model book value, current shares, current
price, consensus or sustainable ROE. The existing fair value, probabilities,
three-leg thesis and model are unchanged. No new rank, buy/sell call or band
is assigned. This is source acquisition and a cited analytical question, not
proof of an investment edge.
