# Does growth translate into operating profit?

All inputs are fictional. Derived figures below use reported values, USD millions, without accounting adjustments.

| Derived measure | Calculation | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FY2025 revenue growth | 120 / 100 - 1 | 20% | [S001, Results](../raw/fy2025-report.md) |
| FY2025 operating margin | 12 / 120 | 10% | [S001, Results](../raw/fy2025-report.md) |
| H1 2026 revenue growth | 66 / 55 - 1 | 20% | [S002, Results](../raw/h1-2026-report.md) |
| H1 2026 operating margin | 5.28 / 66 | 8% | [S002, Results](../raw/h1-2026-report.md) |
| H1 2025 operating margin | 5.5 / 55 | 10% | [S002, Results](../raw/h1-2026-report.md) |
| H1 2026 operating profit growth | 5.28 / 5.5 - 1 | -4% | [S002, Results](../raw/h1-2026-report.md) |

**Supported:** H1 revenue grew while operating profit fell and margin declined by 2 percentage points year over year. [S002, Results](../raw/h1-2026-report.md)

**Inference:** revenue growth alone is insufficient evidence for an improving-profitability thesis. The interim figures weaken that hypothesis but do not settle the full-year outcome. [S002](../raw/h1-2026-report.md)

**Tension, not a resolved contradiction:** the earlier full-year margin outlook and the later interim margin decline cover different periods. A second-half recovery is possible but unsupported by these excerpts. [S001, Management outlook](../raw/fy2025-report.md), [S002](../raw/h1-2026-report.md)

**Missing evidence:** full-year results, accounting definitions, cost breakdown, cash flow, debt, valuation inputs, and explicit guidance update. No valuation or investment recommendation can be derived from this example.
