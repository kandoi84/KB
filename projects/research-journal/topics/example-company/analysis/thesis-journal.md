# Thesis journal — T001: growth and profitability

These entries reconstruct a fictional research sequence on 2026-09-25; they are not contemporaneous historical research records.

## Evidence as of 2026-02-15 — initial view

Prior view: no view recorded.

New evidence: FY2025 revenue and operating profit both grew 20%; margin was 10%. Management expects improvement in 2026. [S001, Results and Management outlook](../raw/fy2025-report.md)

Draft hypothesis: growth may be accompanied by improving profitability. This is an inference based partly on unverified management guidance, with low confidence.

Prove / disprove: comparable 2026 margin above / below 2025's 10% would support / weaken the hypothesis. Next trigger: interim results. Review status: draft.

## Evidence as of 2026-08-15 — weakened view

Prior view: growth may improve profitability (entry above).

New evidence: H1 revenue grew 20%, but operating profit fell 4% and margin declined to 8% from 10%. Calculations appear in [analysis](synthesis.md); input figures in [S002, Results](../raw/h1-2026-report.md).

Revised view: T001 is weakened; interim growth did not produce improved margins. Full-year outlook remains uncertain, not disproven by a half-year result.

Confidence and limits: strong support for the interim arithmetic; low confidence about recovery. Next trigger: full-year results and a cost/guidance update. Comparable full-year margin above / below 10% would support / weaken T001 further. Review status: draft.
