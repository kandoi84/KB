# Reliance Industries — COMPANY.md

## Valuation architecture
Use a sum-of-the-parts framework with separate operating models / DCFs for Jio / Digital Services, Retail, O2C, Oil & Gas / E&P, Media, New Energy, and other material assets when relevant.

Then:
1. Adjust for RIL ownership stakes.
2. Sum attributable enterprise values.
3. Subtract group net debt / other liabilities as appropriate.
4. Apply any holdco / complexity discount only if explicitly justified.
5. Divide by diluted share count to derive fair value per share.

## Reliance-specific analytical priorities

### Jio
Track subscribers, ARPU, 5G monetization, AirFiber/homes, digital-services revenue, EBITDA margin, capex intensity, and IPO/ownership changes.

### Retail
Track revenue growth, EBITDA growth, EBITDA margin, productivity, digital/hyperlocal economics, store footprint, external-channel FMCG growth, working capital and capex.

### O2C
Track refining cracks, realized GRM/product mix, crude differentials, utilization, petchem spreads, feedstock economics, logistics/freight, and turnarounds/outages.

### Oil & Gas
Track KG-D6 volumes, gas/liquids realization, production trend, capex, EBITDA and cash conversion.

### Media
Track revenue growth, advertising, subscription, EBITDA margin and integration/synergy progress.

### New Energy
Treat cautiously until commercial economics are evidenced. Track commissioned capacity, utilization, external sales, EBITDA, capex, ROIC and timing versus management targets.

## Fair-value framework
- Primary anchor: SOTP DCF.
- Cross-check with relevant public-market multiples by segment.
- Do not use consolidated RIL P/E as the primary valuation method.
- Let central fair value move slowly between quarterly model refreshes.
- Maintain price-to-fair-value deviation history and ±1σ / ±2σ bands.

## Trading-band discipline
- Below -1σ: investigate / potential add if thesis intact.
- Below -2σ: high-priority re-underwrite; check for thesis break first.
- Between -1σ and +1σ: neutral valuation zone.
- Above +1σ: investigate / potential trim if estimates have not risen.
- Above +2σ: re-underwrite whether fundamentals justify a new fair-value regime.

## Earnings workflow
1. Pre-earnings preview.
2. Results / transcript deep dive.
3. Segment model update.
4. SOTP / fair-value refresh.
5. Trading-band refresh.
6. Thesis update.
